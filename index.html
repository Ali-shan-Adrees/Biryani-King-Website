Our Story

A Taste of Pakistan in Every Bite

Biryani King began in a family kitchen, where Sunday biryani meant the whole house smelling of fried onions, saffron and slow-cooked masala. We wanted to share that feeling with everyone.

Today we still follow those traditional recipes: fresh meat, premium basmati and hand-ground spices, cooked on dum the proper way.
